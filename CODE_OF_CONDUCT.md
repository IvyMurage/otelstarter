# Code of conduct

OTelStarter is for developers of every experience level, and so is its community. Whether you are filing your first issue or reviewing your hundredth pull request, you should feel welcome here.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient,** especially with people who are new: to the project, to open source, or to observability.
- **Assume good intent.** Ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- **Critique code and ideas, never people.** "This function does two jobs" helps; "you wrote this badly" does not.
- **Accept that people disagree.** Explain your reasoning, listen to theirs, and let the maintainer decide when a choice must be made.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or language.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainer privately through [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/IvyMurage), or through the repository's private security reporting if you prefer. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainer may remove comments, close discussions, or block people who do not follow this code. The aim is always to keep the project a good place to work, not to punish.

This applies in the repository's issues, pull requests and discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.
